    📌 Tips for Reliable Reminders

    ✅ DO:
    • Keep MicroHabit AI in your recent apps
    • Leave app running in background
    • Grant notification permissions
    ❌ DON'T:
    • Swipe away app from recent apps
    • Force close the app
    • Use battery saver mode (may kill app)
    💡 Why?
    Reminders need the app to stay in background to work. This is how browser-based apps function on mobile devices.
